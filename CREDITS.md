# Credits

## Sounds

Nebula's Pomodoro and Clock ring with two real recordings. Both were released into the
public domain by their authors on Wikimedia Commons, so they may be used, changed and
shipped freely, with no conditions. They are credited here anyway, with thanks.

| In Nebula | Recording | Author | Year | License | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Singing bowl (`src/sounds/singing-bowl.ogg`) | "SingingBowl1.ogg" — the sound of a struck singing bowl | BambooBeast | 2008 | Public domain (PD-self) | <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SingingBowl1.ogg> |

The files are shipped as downloaded, unchanged. The same credits are shown in the app under
About Nebula.

## Cities

The World clock and the Weather choose their cities from a list that is in the app
(`src/js/city-data.js`, made by `scripts/build-cities.js`): every city of 15 000 people or
more, with its country, time zone and place. It comes from the GeoNames geographical
database, "cities15000".

| In Nebula | Data | Author | License | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The list of cities | GeoNames "cities15000" — names, countries, time zones, coordinates; some names changed to their English ones | GeoNames | Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) | <https://www.geonames.org/> |

## Weather

The Weather widget shows forecast data by Open-Meteo (<https://open-meteo.com/>), offered
under CC BY 4.0. Their free service is for non-commercial use. Nebula asks it with no
account, no key and no cookies, and sends only the rough place of a city chosen in the widget.
